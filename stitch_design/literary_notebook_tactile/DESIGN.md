## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intimate, tactile digital reading sanctuary modeled after a personal linen-bound reading journal. Crafted specifically for an Android EPUB reader, it bridges the physical tactile pleasure of printed books and ink journaling with the ergonomic demands of modern e-reading.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **handcrafted literary minimalism**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Studious, reflective, quiet, and warm. Avoids mechanical coldness and sterile corporate minimalism while steering clear of whimsical cartoonish tropes.
- **Visual Texture:** Rich ivory paper substrates, crisp 1.5px charcoal ink contours with subtle organic variance, hard mechanical stamp offsets, and tactile reading aids (ribbon bookmarks, marginalia flags, inked divider lines).
- **Core Principles:** 
  1. *Subservience to Text:* Controls and framing elements recede gently into warm neutral backgrounds so reader immersion remains uninterrupted.
  2. *Artisan Precision:* Structural lines echo hand-ruled fountain pen ink on heavy vellum stock.
  3. *Ergonomic Android Native Utility:* Conforms rigorously to accessible 48dp minimum touch targets, thumb-driven navigation zones, and clear visual state signifiers.

## Colors

The color system emulates natural physical printing materials: raw bleached pulp, carbon charcoal ink, dried plant pigment, and terracotta bookcloth.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Paper Surface (`#FAF8F2`):** Primary canvas reflecting natural, unbleached book pages with lowered blue-light emission for prolonged reading sessions.
- **Charcoal Ink (`#292724`):** Primary ink for high-contrast reading text, precise line rules, and interactive element outlines.
- **Muted Sage (`#83917A`):** Secondary accent reserved for reading progress, reading session indicators, active navigation tabs, and peaceful annotation categories.
- **Soft Ochre / Terracotta (`#C87D55`):** Tertiary accent for critical highlights, bookmark tags, active scrubbers, and primary action affordances.
- **Paper Containers (`#F2EBE0`, `#E8E0D2`):** Grounded background surfaces used to isolate sheets, reader settings cards, drawer menus, and TOC panels without modern artificial blur or color contamination.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Newsreader** (an optical editorial serif built for long-form literary readability) with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (a crisp, humanist sans-serif providing friction-free clarity on technical UI controls).

- **Headlines & Literary Body:** Set in *Newsreader* with generous line spacing to prevent eye fatigue. It conveys the authority and grace of classical book composition.
- **Controls, Meta & Status Indicators:** Set in *Plus Jakarta Sans* to clearly demarcate the reading interface apparatus (page numbers, estimated time remaining, font settings, battery status) from the literary text itself.
- **Numbers:** Tabular lining numbers are used for EPUB percentages, chapter timestamps, and pagination indicators to ensure vertical stability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around responsive page canvases optimized for reading flow and one-handed thumb interaction.

- **Mobile (Phone):** Fluid vertical flow with standard `1rem` screen margin and `1rem` gutters. Interactive toolbars, chapter selectors, and reading drawer drawers pin to the bottom sheet zone to maximize thumb reach.
- **Tablet / Foldable:** Dual-page spread simulation with expanded `2rem` outer margins and a distinct vertical ink-ruled gutter (`1.5rem`) mimicking a book gutter.
- **Touch Targets:** All actionable elements strictly maintain a minimum boundary of 48×48dp, even when ink lines visually indicate a smaller pill or button outline, by leveraging invisible outer touch-target expansions.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects translucent frosted blurs, soft diffused lighting, and glossy elevation gradients. Depth is established via tactile paper layering and crisp, offset physical stamping:

1. **Hard Offset Ink Shadows:** Floating surfaces (bottom sheets, dropdown menus, reading control cards, floating bookmarks) utilize a crisp hard offset shadow:
   - Elevation Low: `1.5px 1.5px 0px #292724`
   - Elevation High: `3px 3px 0px #292724`
2. **Paper Tiering:** Background levels step from base reading paper (`#FAF8F2`) to panel container (`#F2EBE0`) to elevated flyout sheets (`#E8E0D2`).
3. **Inked Dividers:** Section cutoffs and hierarchy separations use solid `1.5px` ink borders (`#292724`), occasionally punctuated with stitched dash segments (`border-style: dashed`) to simulate ledger paper bindings.

## Shapes

The shape system evokes trimmed book board, cut scrap paper, and hand-sketched notebook elements.

- **Hand-Drawn Organic Radii:** Primary cards, sheet containers, and key interactive components use subtle, asymmetrical organic corner radii (e.g. `border-radius: 255px 15px 225px 15px / 15px 225px 15px 255px` or simplified asymmetrical variations like `4px 6px 3px 5px`). This introduces an authentic, human feel without creating distracting visual imbalance.
- **Base Geometry:** Structural base roundedness is kept subtle (`roundedness: 1`), keeping lines taut and disciplined.
- **Ink Contours:** All interactive containers feature a visible `1.5px` solid Charcoal Ink border (`#292724`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `#292724` Charcoal Ink with `#FAF8F2` text. Solid `1.5px` outline, with a `2px 2px 0px #83917A` offset shadow. On press, the button shifts `2px 2px`, collapsing the shadow to simulate physical pressure.
- **Secondary (Paper Outline):** `#FAF8F2` background, `1.5px` solid `#292724` ink stroke, `2px 2px 0px #292724` offset shadow.
- **Text / Bookmark Action:** Borderless label in `#C87D55` or `#83917A` with an underlined stroke styled like an ink pen rule.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact paper pills bordered with `1.5px #292724`. Unselected state uses `#FAF8F2` with subtle organic asymmetrical corners (`6px 4px 5px 3px`). Selected state shifts to `#83917A` with crisp `#FAF8F2` type, dropping a `1.5px 1.5px 0px #292724` shadow.

### Cards & Book Items
- Library and metadata cards utilize `#F2EBE0` container backgrounds framed by a `1.5px` ink border and `2px 2px 0px #292724` drop shadow.
- Book covers display an embossed spine line along the left edge (`2px solid rgba(41, 39, 36, 0.15)`) replicating sewn library binding.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Text Inputs:** Paper-lined inset boxes with `1.5px` ink border, `#F2EBE0` fill, and font set to *Plus Jakarta Sans*. Focus states feature a `2px 2px 0px #C87D55` highlight shadow.
- **Reading Sliders (Brightness / Font Size):** Custom track consisting of a continuous `2px` charcoal line. The slider thumb is a paper square rotated 45 degrees (diamond) or hand-stamped circle bordered in `2px` ink and filled with `#FAF8F2`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** Square ink frame (`18×18dp` visual, enclosed in `48×48dp` touch target) with slightly uneven corners (`2px 3px 2px 3px`). When checked, displays an authentic hand-drawn ink checkmark in `#292724` or `#C87D55`.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular ink outline containing a solid ink center dot when selected.

### Bottom Sheet (Reader Quick-Settings & TOC)
- Drawn from `#FAF8F2` with a top `2px` solid ink border. Features a hand-sketched top drag handle (`32px` wide, `3px` thick ink stroke with rounded ends). Includes full chapter progression lists separated by dotted ink dividers.
