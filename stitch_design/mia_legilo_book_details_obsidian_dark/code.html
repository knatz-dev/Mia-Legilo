Curator's Synopsis

format_quote

Shevek, a brilliant physicist from the arid anarchist moon of Anarres, risks ostracism and treason to travel to the lavish mother planet Urras. In seeking to share his revolutionary General Temporal Theory, he attempts to bridge two estranged worlds separated by ideology, walled borders, and centuries of reciprocal suspicion.

Le Guin constructs an astonishing meditation on human freedom, property, and exile. Contrast between the collective hardship of the Odonian settlers and the decadent, patriarchal splendor of A-Io provides the canvas for an unforgettable exploration of what it truly means to possess nothing—and therefore possess oneself.
