## Brand & Style
This design system defines an immersive, tactile sepia reading sanctuary inspired by private libraries, antique vellum manuscripts, and natural honey parchment. Built for discerning readers, literary researchers, and contemplative writers, the interface evokes calm focus, physical warmth, and organic intimacy.

The aesthetic fuses literary editorial minimalism with subtle skeuomorphic tactility:
- **Parchment Immersion:** Soft, warm honey substrates emulate heavyweight archival paper, reducing eye fatigue during marathon reading sessions.
- **Inkcraft Precision:** Hand-drawn discipline with soft-ground etching lines, deckled-edge divider nuances, and ribbon bookmarker cues.
- **Literary Serenity:** Gentle visual hierarchy devoid of synthetic harshness, creating an atmosphere reminiscent of oil-lamp lit study chambers.

## Colors
The palette abandons stark contrast in favor of natural pigment values derived from iron gall ink, terracotta silk, sun-dried parchment, and dried garden herbs.

- **Primary (`#382c1e`):** Deep umber ink. Anchors reading text, primary icon strokes, and fundamental structural lines.
- **Secondary (`#be664b`):** Warm terracotta silk ribbon. Used sparingly for active bookmarks, focal points, and progress markers.
- **Tertiary (`#6f7d66`):** Soft olive sage. Designates margin annotations, completed states, tags, and subtle secondary actions.
- **Neutral Parchment Foundations:** 
  - Canvas / Surface Base: `#f4ecd8` (soft honey vellum)
  - Surface Raised / Container: `#ede3ca` (aged pressed linen paper)
  - Surface Sunken / Inset Margins: `#e5d9bd` (deep parchment grain)
- **Ink Outlines & Borders:**
  - Delicate Border: `#c4b59b` (subtle wash outline)
  - Emphasized Border: `#a8977c` (sepia graphite line)
- **Secondary & Tertiary Typographic Tones:**
  - Body Secondary: `#544432` (soft warm walnut)
  - Metadata / Captions: `#6e5c47` (muted umber dust)

## Typography
`Newsreader` is employed uniformly across all typographic scales to mirror continuous classical book typesetting. Its variable optical sizes preserve legibility at micro annotations while maintaining graceful organic chiseled serifs at display sizes.

- **Proportions:** Generous body line heights (1.65 to 1.75 ratio) ensure effortless horizontal eye travel, preventing line-skipping across long passages.
- **Rhythm:** Italics are used intentionally for marginal notes, pull quotes, translation hints, and secondary citations rather than relying on heavy weights.
- **Numerals:** Old-style figures are preferred in body reading settings to balance seamlessly alongside lowercase letterforms.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture models classical book margins: generous, breathable outer borders that invite the thumbs to rest comfortably without occluding text.

- **Editorial Reading Column:** Central literary text tracks a fixed comfortable reading measure between 60 to 72 characters per line (max-width `42rem` to `48rem`).
- **Marginalia Gutter:** On desktop screens, an auxiliary outer gutter (column width `16rem`) accommodates olive sage marginal notes, footnotes, glossaries, and reader reflections.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Desktop (1200px+):* Wide book layout with dual page simulation or asymmetrical marginalia zone.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1199px):* Single unified central column with collapsible collapsible flyout margin notes.
  - *Mobile (<768px):* Compact leaf layout with condensed edge margins (`1.25rem`) maximizing printable canvas.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects synthetic floating drop-shadows and cold blur-heavy glassmorphism in favor of tactile paper layering and subtle ambient light.

- **Tonal Deckling:** Elevation is communicated through tone shifts across paper surfaces. A background canvas of `#f4ecd8` hosts content cards of `#ede3ca`, while recessed reading wells or quotation callouts rest on `#e5d9bd`.
- **Soft Pressed Paper Shadows:** When elevation is strictly required (such as floating bottom toolbars or modal reader menus), use soft, warm-tinted ambient occlusion:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(56, 44, 30, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(56, 44, 30, 0.04)`
- **Sepia Ink Outlines:** Floating elements and interactive cards are circumscribed with a 1px soft hairline border (`#c4b59b`), mimicking a precision pen rule on bookbinding linen.
- **Layered Page Inset:** Inset reading areas employ a delicate top inner shade (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(56, 44, 30, 0.06)`) mimicking the curvature of an open bound book spine.

## Shapes
The shape language draws from cut vellum sheets, hand-bound leather spines, and naturally softened book corners.

- **Corner Radius:** Standard components utilize a gentle `0.25rem` (`roundedness: 1`), conveying natural organic softness rather than mechanical sharpness or synthetic hyper-roundedness.
- **Large Panels & Book Plates:** Containers, modals, and parchment plates scale gracefully to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Ribbon & Bookmark Elements:** Flag markers and bookmark notches use geometric angular tail cutouts (`45-degree inward cut`) in terracotta `#be664b`.
- **Dividers & Rules:** Horizontal separators are styled as delicate variable-width ink strokes (0.75px to 1px) with tapered organic endpoints.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button (Terracotta Ribbon):** Background `#be664b`, text `#f4ecd8`, hairline border `#a8977c`. Hover state deepens to `#a8563e`. Conveys physical touch with a subtle 1px downward translation on press.
- **Secondary Button (Archival Paper):** Background `#ede3ca`, text `#382c1e`, 1px solid `#c4b59b`. Hover subtly warms to `#e5d9bd`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, text `#544432`, underlined with a delicate dotted rule in `#a8977c`.

### Text Inputs & Search
- Background `#f4ecd8`, border 1px solid `#c4b59b`, interior padding `space-sm` `space-md`.
- Typography styled in `Newsreader` italic for placeholders (`#6e5c47`). Focused state introduces an umber boundary rule (`#382c1e`) and a soft terracotta focus halo.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 16px squares with soft `0.125rem` corners, 1.5px ink border in `#544432`, filled with `#6f7d66` on select, bearing a bone-colored checkmark.
- Radio Buttons: Hand-drawn circular appearance with solid umber center dot upon activation.

### Cards & Reading Plates
- Canvas `#ede3ca`, encapsulated by a 1px border `#c4b59b`.
- Header separator: 1px hairline rule `#e5d9bd` across the plate width.
- Padding structured strictly with `space-md` or `space-lg`.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill or softened rectangles with background `#e5d9bd`, text `#544432`, and outline `#c4b59b`.
- Sage category pills: `#6f7d66` background with `#f4ecd8` text for active chapter filters or subject tags.

### Specialized Literary Components
- **Marginal Note (Scholia):** Positioned in desktop gutters or expandable accordions; typeset in `body-sm` italic with tertiary olive sage (`#6f7d66`) and anchored via delicate superscript indices.
- **Silk Ribbon Bookmark:** Floating corner bookmark tab in `#be664b` fixed along the top right edge of current reading sections.
- **Chapter Pull Quote:** Centered plate framed top and bottom by twin delicate sepia rules with a small centered fleuron or asterisk glyph.
