THE DISPOSSESSED CHAPTER 1 • P. 14

There was a wall. It did not look important. It was built of un-cut rocks, roughly mortared. An adult could look right over it, and even a child could climb it. Where it crossed the roadway, instead of having a gate it simply dwindled to a foot-wide gap.

The wall had been built to keep people out, or maybe to keep them in. To Anarres, the wall enclosed a small piece of land around the spaceport, shutting off the world from the ships of Urras. To Urras, the wall enclosed the entire planet Anarres, shutting it off from the rest of the cosmos.

Like all walls it was ambiguous, two-faced. What was inside it and what was outside it depended upon which side of it you were on.
